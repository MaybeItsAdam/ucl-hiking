The UCL Hiking Club app puts the whole club in your pocket: every walk on the programme, the club's routes, your club kit, The Navigator and your own hiking year, signed in with your UCL account.

EVERY WALK, PROPERLY BRIEFED
Each walk has its own page with the distance, total ascent, difficulty grade, train and fare, meeting point and time, and who's leading and backmarking. The route sits on an Ordnance Survey map, with an elevation profile you can scrub along to see the day's climbs before you commit.

EVERY CLUB ROUTE
Routes is a catalogue of the club's OS Maps library. Search by name or filter by distance, then open any route for its map, elevation profile and GPX download, and see the walks that have used it.

YOUR HIKING YEAR
My Walks shows the trips you're signed up for and the ones you've done, with this year's distance and climbing totals and a map of everywhere you've been. The club walk map shows the whole year's routes, including the longest day and the biggest climb.

NEVER MISS A WALK
Subscribe to the club calendar so new walks appear in your phone's calendar app. Turn on notifications to hear about announcements and changes, and catch up on anything you missed in your inbox.

BORROW CLUB KIT
Browse the equipment store, from rucksacks and tents to waterproofs, with live stock and condition. Request a loan for your dates and trip, then follow the request from pending to on loan to returned.

THE NAVIGATOR
Every issue of The Navigator, the club magazine, is in the app. The reader turns pages like a printed copy, with facing pages on a wide screen, and you can download any issue to keep.

FOR WALK LEADERS
Claim walk slots on the leader rota, see which walks still need a leader, and keep the walk-day register, emergency details and booked kit on your phone, even when signal drops. Read the leader handbook, file expense claims, and see how many walks and kilometres each leader has covered.

FOR THE COMMITTEE
Manage the inventory and loans, the membership roster, the walk programme, incident reports, announcements and club money, all from your phone. What you see depends on your club role.

SIGN IN WITH UCL
Sign in with your UCL account through Adam's Campus Toolbox. Your access follows your Students' Union membership of the club. If you aren't a member yet, the app shows you how to join.

An internet connection is required for most features.
