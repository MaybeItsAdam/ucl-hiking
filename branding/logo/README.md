# Procedural app mark

The app icon is generated by `scripts/logo/generate.mjs` from a seed. The same
seed always gives the same mark, and a new seed re-rolls it.

**Live icon: seed 7, no contours.** Its SVGs are the masters
`branding/app_icons/app-icon.svg` and `app-icon-foreground.svg`, and
`scripts/generate-app-icons.sh` renders those into every live location. To
change the icon:

```sh
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed N --export
cp branding/logo/out/N/svg/icon.svg branding/app_icons/app-icon.svg
cp branding/logo/out/N/svg/adaptive-foreground.svg branding/app_icons/app-icon-foreground.svg
scripts/generate-app-icons.sh
cp assets/store/google-play-icon.png fastlane/metadata/android/en-GB/images/icon.png
cp assets/store/google-play-icon.png assets/store/android/app-icon.png
cp assets/store/apple-app-icon.png assets/store/ios/app-icon.png
```

The last three copies cover store files that the shell script doesn't write.

Every seed is drawn in the club crest's mountain style (`branding/Logo.png`, the
live icon, the Play feature graphic). The seed only varies things inside that
style.

## How a mark is built

1. **Peaks.** There are always three straight-edged triangular peaks. A dominant
   summit, with a long gentle outer flank and a steep inner one, steps down to
   two lower peaks. The seed sets each apex position and each flank slope within
   narrow ranges. There is no roughness or displacement: every edge is a
   straight line.
2. **Overlaps.** Where two peaks meet, a slit of sky cuts down between them, as
   on the crest. The slit runs down the front peak's flank to a seeded tip, then
   climbs back up a steep back edge until it meets the rear peak's flank. If a
   seed's slit would cut past the next apex, it is made shallower until it fits.
3. **Snow caps.** Each cap is an arrowhead whose edges run parallel to the rock,
   set in from it by a thin rim. Its base is notched about a third of the way
   across, and the long-flank foot drops lower, which is the crest's own cap
   shape. The depth is a seeded share of the peak's visible height, with a
   minimum.
4. **Trail.** A slim lime trail with even dashes runs from a fixed trailhead to a
   fixed end at the foot of the summit snow. Between them it follows a Brownian
   bridge (a random walk pinned at both ends), smoothed and mixed with a seeded
   number of switchbacks. It is kept on the rock, out of the first slit and
   inside the safe circle.
5. **Contours (optional, `--contours`).** Five copies of the ridge, offset
   downwards, smoothed and flattened progressively and clipped to the rock.
6. **Mirror.** Half the seeds flip the whole scene, putting the summit on the
   right.

The seed controls shape only. Colour, peak count, trail ends and contrast are
fixed. The sky is the brand teal `#01A2A6` (`branding/brand.md`), which is also
the manifest `theme_color` and Android `ic_launcher_background`. It is
deliberately not the UI accent `--forest` `#08a8ad`. The rest comes from
`src/app/globals.css`: rock `--deep` `#2d4153`, snow white, and trail `--lime`
`#b9da3b`.
Contours are `#4b6a84`, the rock lifted toward `--ink-fill`. All fills are flat,
with no gradients, no text and no compass.

## Safe area

The geometry lives in a 1024 design space. All meaningful content stays inside
a circle of about r = 300 around the centre. The rock runs past the bottom and
sides, so a mask only ever trims rock or sky.

- **Android adaptive foreground**: the scene is scaled ×0.85 (`ADAPTIVE_SCALE`)
  into the 108dp layer. The circle that is guaranteed to be visible is 66dp
  (r = 313 at 1024), and the Android 12+ launcher and splash show r = 341. At
  1:1 the outer snow cap reached r ≈ 362 and was clipped. At ×0.85 it ends at
  r ≈ 307. `scripts/generate-splash.sh` reads the scale back from the SVG, so
  the flat-base splash is unaffected.
- **iOS, web, legacy Android, PWA maskable**: the scene is scaled ×1.3, which
  keeps the mark inside the 40% maskable zone and clear of the iOS corner mask.
- **Adaptive background**: a flat sky layer, also written as
  `@color/ic_launcher_background`.

## Commands

```sh
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed 7                     # full icon SVG to stdout
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed 7 --contours          # ...with contour lines
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed 7 --layer foreground  # adaptive foreground only
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed 7 --params            # the parameters the seed rolled
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --sheet --seeds 1-8 --out /path/sheet.png   # each seed with and without contours
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --compare 7,1,5 --out /path/vs.png          # against the live icon, 180 and 29px
node scripts/logo/generate.mjs --seed 7 --export [--contours]              # every size -> branding/logo/out/7/
```

The sheets need headless Chrome (set `CHROME=` if it's not in the usual place).
Export needs `rsvg-convert` and `magick`, the same tools as
`scripts/generate-app-icons.sh`.

`--export` writes the following into `branding/logo/out/<seed>/`, which is
gitignored staging:

- `svg/`: the full icon plus the adaptive foreground and background layers.
- `web/`: `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`
  (180, opaque), `icon-192/512.png`, `icon-maskable-192/512.png` and `icon.svg`.
- `ios/AppIcon.appiconset/`: a single 1024 universal icon (opaque) and
  `Contents.json`, in the same shape as the live catalog.
- `android/res/mipmap-{m,h,xh,xxh,xxxh}dpi/`: `ic_launcher`, `ic_launcher_round`,
  `ic_launcher_foreground` and `ic_launcher_background`, plus
  `mipmap-anydpi-v26/*.xml`, `values/ic_launcher_background.xml` and
  `android/playstore-icon.png` (512).

## Shortlist

`top/` holds the full, adaptive-foreground and with-contours SVGs for each seed.

| Seed | Why |
|---|---|
| **7** | The closest to the live icon: a big summit on the left with two clean, deep slits stepping down to the right. It keeps its three-peak silhouette at 29px. |
| **1** | The same family with a broader summit cap and a gentler second slit. It is the calmest and most even of the set. |
| **5** | The mirrored option: the summit sits on the right and the trail sweeps up from the left. Pick it if the icon should face the other way. |

**Contours: recommend leaving them off.** They vanish below about 60px. At 180
and 1024 they compete with the trail and blur the crest's flat, large-plane
look. The crest itself has none.

## Splash screens

The native launch splash is a flat brand-teal `#01A2A6` field with the mark
centred: the adaptive foreground's peaks, snow caps and trail standing on a flat
base, where the rock is cut off by a straight line 24 units under the trailhead.
Teal, because it is the icon's sky and the rock is slate; on a slate field only
the snow and trail would show. The PWA manifest's `background_color` is the same
teal.

```sh
scripts/generate-splash.sh                     # rewrite every splash image
scripts/generate-splash.sh --preview out.png   # ...plus an iPhone / Android port / Android 12+ / land preview
```

It reads `branding/app_icons/app-icon-foreground.svg`, so re-run it after
changing the icon. It writes:

- iOS `ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/Splash.imageset/splash-2732x2732{,-1,-2}.png`:
  one 2732 square, drawn aspect-fill by `LaunchScreen.storyboard`. The mark is
  16% of the square wide, which is about 35% of an iPhone's width and 21% of an
  iPad's short side once cropped.
- Android `android/app/src/main/res/drawable-{m,h,xh,xxh,xxxh}dpi/splash_mark.png`:
  the transparent mark, 120dp wide (30% of a 400dp phone).
  `drawable/splash.xml` layers it, centred and unscaled, over
  `@color/splash_background`, so it keeps its shape in either orientation.
  That is the launch theme's `android:background` before Android 12.
- Android 12+ draws its own splash from `windowSplashScreenBackground`
  (`@color/splash_background` in `values/colors.xml`) and
  `windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon` (`@mipmap/ic_launcher`, which the system crops
  to a circle), both set on `AppTheme.NoActionBarLaunch` in `values/styles.xml`.

Change the teal in the script, `values/colors.xml` and `src/app/manifest.ts`
together.
