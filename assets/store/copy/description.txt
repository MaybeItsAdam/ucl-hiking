Get ready for your next adventure with UCL Hiking Club.

BROWSE CLUB KIT
See the club equipment catalogue, check stock and condition, and filter by category to find the kit you need.

REQUEST AN EQUIPMENT LOAN
Choose the quantity, borrowing dates and trip details, then send a request for committee review. Check your own requests and loan status in the member portal.

TOOLS FOR THE COMMITTEE
Authorised club officers can manage inventory, review equipment requests and keep track of active loans. Features depend on your club role.

SIGN IN WITH UCL
Use your UCL account through Adam's Campus Toolbox. Equipment requests are available to eligible Standard and Explorer members; access depends on your current club membership.

An internet connection is required.
